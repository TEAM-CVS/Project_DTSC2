What the score means

The target is positive surplus WAR next season: actual production above the position’s expected production at that cap share.

An 85% model output is a ranking signal. Calibration was not evaluated in the notebook, so it should not be read as a verified 85% chance.

This does not predict whether a team actually retains a player. It estimates whether the player will provide positive modeled value.

Validation & limitations

Time-based split

922 training examples use feature seasons through 2021. The 284 test examples use later feature seasons. The price curve uses outcome seasons through 2022.

Uncertainty remains

1,000 test-set bootstrap resamples give an AUC gain interval that includes zero. A clear improvement over the original is not established.

Further checks needed

Replacement baselines, wins-per-EPA, and shrinkage priors use pooled data. Refit all preprocessing within each training fold before treating this as a fully isolated forecast evaluation.

The analytical pipeline

Clean and attribute plays

2015–2024 nflverse play-by-play via nflreadpy. Regular season pass/run plays only; remove deleted plays, kneels, spikes, and two-point attempts. Split targeted-pass EPA 50/50 between QB and target; designed runs go to the rusher.

Estimate WAR

Aggregate credited EPA to player-seasons. Define starters by play volume: 1 QB, 1 RB, 3 WRs, and 1 TE per team-season. Compare players with pooled non-starter EPA/play at their position, then multiply EPA above replacement by approximately 0.02634 wins per EPA.

Put production in cost context

Flatten OverTheCap season history, deduplicate player-season-team rows, and sum across teams. Cap share is expressed in percentage points: 2.6 means 2.6% of the cap. Fit expected WAR against cap share separately for each position.

Score next-season surplus

Use up to three seasons of shrunk rates, WAR, play volume, cap share, rookie-deal proxy, experience, draft round, undrafted flag, and position. Fit balanced logistic regression; refit on all labeled seasons to score qualified 2024 players.

CSV data is read in this browser session. Reloading restores the supplied notebook excerpt. The recommender filters and ranks your scored output in the browser; it does not run or retrain the Python model. Dollar budgets require cap_number, exported in $ millions.

Read the board responsibly

The excerpt includes dollar cap hits for only 3 players. Other dollar costs are unknown and are excluded from dollar-budget searches. No costs are inferred from rounded cap percentages. Import the full CSV for complete dollar-budget recommendations.

The bundled data contains the top five and bottom five displayed scores at each position: 40 selected 2024 player-seasons, rounded as saved in the updated notebook. Averages and ranks apply only to the loaded selection.

“High,” “Monitor,” and “Review” are UI sorting tiers: ≥60%, 40–60%, and <40%. They are not validated keep/release thresholds. Rookie deal is the notebook’s first-four-seasons proxy.

Team is the player’s main team in that historical season. Injuries, future contract changes, guarantees, and release savings are not modeled here.
